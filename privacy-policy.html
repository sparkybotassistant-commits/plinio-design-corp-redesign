Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Plinio Design Corp (“PDC,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website, contacting our business, or submitting inquiries through our Facebook and Instagram advertisements.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact Plinio Design Corp or request a renovation estimate, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address or project location
  • Type of renovation or construction services requested
  • Estimated project budget and timeline
  • Photos, messages, or other project details voluntarily submitted

We may receive this information directly through our website, by telephone, by email, or through lead forms on Facebook and Instagram.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to inquiries and estimate requests
  • Contact prospective customers regarding renovation projects
  • Schedule consultations and appointments
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, and contracts
  • Provide construction and renovation services
  • Manage customer relationships and improve our services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We may contact you using the information you provide regarding your requested services. Separate consent will be obtained for promotional communications when required by law.

4. Facebook and Instagram Advertising

Plinio Design Corp uses Meta advertising services, including Facebook and Instagram, to promote our construction and renovation services.

When you submit a lead form through one of our advertisements, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project, and provide estimates.

Meta also processes information under its own privacy policies, available at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with authorized employees and service providers who assist with website hosting, communications, customer management, advertising administration, and the delivery of our services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

We do not authorize service providers to use customer inquiry information for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, and meet legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact Plinio Design Corp to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable laws and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

You may also request that we stop sending promotional communications.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including Facebook, Instagram, and other platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party services. We encourage visitors to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults seeking construction and renovation services. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 18 through our advertising lead forms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business operations, website practices, or applicable laws.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
